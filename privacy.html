Privacy overview.

This page is a plain-language overview of the intended privacy approach for MY DOSO. It is not a substitute for the final, legally reviewed privacy policy that should accompany a public release.

Local-first everyday information

MY DOSO is designed so core personal organising features — such as tasks, reminders, notes and calendar information — are stored and managed on the user's device. The app is not designed around a required account or cloud synchronisation in its initial release.

AI Assistant

AI features require an internet connection. When you use the assistant, relevant conversation content and context may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Avoid entering passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information into the assistant.

Permissions and notifications

Some optional capabilities may request Android permissions, such as notification, alarm, overlay or usage-access permissions. These should be requested only when the related feature is used, with a clear explanation. You can manage app permissions through Android settings.

Backups

If you use the app's manual backup or export feature, the resulting file is under your control. Store and share backup files carefully because they may contain personal information.

Changes and contact

This overview may change as MY DOSO develops. Before public launch, Dosani Labs should publish a complete policy describing the final data handling, retention, third-party services and contact details.
